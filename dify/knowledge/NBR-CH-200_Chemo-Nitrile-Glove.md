- **Sizes:** S, M, L, XL
- **Standards:** ASTM D6978 (tested against chemotherapy drugs), ASTM D6319, EN 455, EN ISO 374-1 Type B
- **AQL (pinholes):** 1.0
- **Packing:** 50 gloves per box
