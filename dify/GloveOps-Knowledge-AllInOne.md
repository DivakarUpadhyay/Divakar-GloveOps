# FAQ — choosing a glove (fictional demo data)

**Nitrile vs vinyl vs latex vs TPE?**
Nitrile (NBR) is strong, chemical-resistant and latex-free — best for medical and lab use. Vinyl (PVC) is the cheapest, looser fit, for food and light tasks. Latex fits best but can cause allergies. TPE is a low-cost, recyclable food glove.

**What does AQL mean?**
Acceptable Quality Limit: the maximum pinhole defect rate allowed in sampling. Lower is better. Medical exam gloves are typically AQL 1.5; chemo gloves 1.0.

**Which glove for chemotherapy drugs?** NBR-CH-200, tested to ASTM D6978.

**Which glove for a semiconductor cleanroom?** CR-NBR-100 (ISO Class 5, low particle, ESD-safe).

**Which glove for a restaurant?** PVC-FD-100 (lowest cost) or TPE-FD-100 (better fit, recyclable).

**How do I request samples?** Sales creates a sample request in GloveOps. It is approved by the Sales Manager and QA; requests of 10,000 boxes or more also need the plant manager.

**Lead time for samples?** Usually 7–14 days after final approval, plus shipping.
